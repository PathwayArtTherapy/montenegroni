Getting There

Fly into Tivat TIV

Our recommended airport

Tivat is the closest airport to Perast, roughly 25–30 minutes by car. We can organise a shuttle to collect you from Tivat airport.

Fancy a road trip?

  • Dubrovnik, Croatia DBV About 1.5–2 hours by road, including a border crossing (allow extra time in summer).
  • Podgorica TGD About 1.5–2 hours by road.

Getting around Perast

Perast is a mostly car-free town. If you rent a car, there's paid parking up the hill at the edge of town (it can be a bit pricey). Otherwise, the shuttle from Tivat is the easiest option.

FAQ

Do I need a visa?

Montenegro isn't part of the EU or the Schengen Area. Australian passport holders don't need a visa for short visits (up to 90 days), and time spent in Montenegro doesn't count towards your Schengen days, which is handy if you're combining the trip with Europe. If you're travelling on another passport, please check the entry rules for your nationality.

What currency is used?

Montenegro uses the euro (€).

Is Perast hilly?

From our house there's a slight hill down into town, but once you're in town everything is relatively flat. Leaving Perast is hilly… unless you go by sea!

Can I bring my kids?

Kids are very welcome at the celebration! The house we've booked is adults only, so families will need to arrange their own accommodation. Just ask us if you'd like suggestions.

Do I have to come for the whole week?

Not at all. Come for the week, a few days, or just the Saturday celebration. Whatever works for you.

What should I wear?

It's relaxed. For the celebration, the theme is Euro summer cocktail — colourful! Pack swimwear, sunscreen and comfy shoes.
