Getting around Perast

Perast town itself is flat, so once you're down by the water it's an easy stroll everywhere. Getting into town means heading down a hill, so if you're staying up on the hill, expect a climb on the way home. Perast is mostly car-free. If you rent a car, there's paid parking up the hill at the edge of town (it can be a bit pricey).

FAQ

Do I need a visa?

Montenegro isn't part of the EU or the Schengen Area. Australian passport holders don't need a visa for short visits (up to 90 days), and time spent in Montenegro doesn't count towards your Schengen days, which is handy if you're combining the trip with Europe. If you're travelling on another passport, please check the entry rules for your nationality.

What currency is used?

Montenegro uses the euro (€).

Is Perast hilly?

Perast town itself is flat. Getting into town is down a hill (and back up again afterwards), and if you stay up on the hill you'll get your steps in. Leaving Perast is hilly… unless you go by sea!

Can I bring my kids?

Kids are very welcome at the celebration! The house we've booked is adults only, so families will need to arrange their own accommodation. Just ask us if you'd like suggestions.

Do I have to come for the whole week?

Not at all. Come for the week, a few days, or just the Saturday celebration. Whatever works for you.

What should I wear?

It's relaxed. For the celebration, the theme is Euro summer chic — colours, silks, flowy. Top 3 buttons open. Pack swimwear, sunscreen and comfy shoes.
